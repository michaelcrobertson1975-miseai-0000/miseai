MiseAI · The House PaperMonday Morning

A warm night, a good close, and one number to watch.

Yesterday closed at $3,704 across 88 covers, average check $42.09. Prime Cost finished at 58.4% — food at 28.1%, labor at 30.3%.

That's your fourth day below the 8-week baseline of 60.3%. Volume has been a little softer this week, but the operation is holding its margins. The crew kept a good pace through service, averaging $529 in sales for every hour the doors were open.

The number I'd look at this morning

Chicken breast is up 18.4% — your latest delivery moved from $4.28 to $5.07/lb. At your usual 40 pounds a week, that's about $31.60 more every week if the price holds.

Nothing to change today. I'd ask the vendor whether this is a temporary move. If it sticks, then we look at the menu cost.

Tonight looks different

It's going to be hot early, and people will be looking for a cooler room, a cold drink, and somewhere comfortable to get out of the heat. That changes the shape of the night — I'd expect the early rush to come sooner, with guests settling into air conditioning or a shaded table rather than lingering outside.

Don't prep for yesterday's night. Watch the first part of service and let tonight's sales tell you where the demand is actually going.

One other thing caught my eye

Chicken usage was 25% above your normal pattern over the last 48 hours. That doesn't automatically mean waste. Before changing anything, I'd check the portion and prep yield. If those are normal, look at the sales mix — if chicken dishes simply sold more, the number may be completely explainable. If they weren't responsible for the increase, we've found something worth following.

One thing MiseAI refused to guess

Two invoice lines were too unclear to read confidently. They're not being used to distort your food cost — they're sitting in your confirmation queue for a quick review. That's intentional. If MiseAI doesn't know, it tells you.
