Your restaurant starter kit.

Use what you already have. These worksheets help you spot the few details we may need to ask about.

You do not need to finish these in one sitting. Print a blank copy, or enter notes and print to keep them. Entries on this worksheet are not saved online.

1. Start with your supplier order sheet.

A current supplier sheet is best. Keep the original product codes, pack descriptions, prices, and price date. If anything is unclear, mark it for review.

Supplier / product codeProductPack count × size / unitPrice / priced unitPrice date

For example, write the measured contents of a case—not just “1 case.” This worksheet is for preparation; it is not an automatic import format.

2. Bring one recipe.

Choose a dish you know well. A batch of sauce or another subrecipe is a good place to begin.

Weight ounces and fluid ounces are different. Do not guess a conversion. Record whether trimming or cooking loss is already included in your measured yield.

Draft worksheet · Not approved for inventory consumption

3. Gather the supporting records.

  • Recent invoices, including all pages.
  • Sales / POS reports with item codes and reporting dates.
  • Your current menu and portion or modifier options.
  • A dated opening inventory count, when agreed with Michael.

AI can help read supplied information and identify questions. Review product matches, measurements, and recipe drafts before approving them. It should never fill missing measurements with guesses.
